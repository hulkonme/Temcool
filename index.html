    FAQ

    Good questions, straight answers.

    Is Temcool actually receiving real email?

    Yes. Every address is a live mailbox on the mail.tm network. Mail sent from any provider — Gmail, Outlook, a sign-up form — is delivered to the inbox on this page.

    Can I choose my own address?

    Absolutely. Type a name in the “Make it yours” field and pick one of the available domains. If it's taken, try another.

    How long does an inbox last?

    The timer resets to 60 minutes every time you press Extend. Your session stays linked to this browser for six hours. Generate a fresh address whenever you want to start over.

    Can I send email from it?

    No — Temcool is receive-only by design. It's built for verification links, sign-up confirmations and one-off messages.

    Is it private?

    Anyone who knows the address can email it, and the inbox is tied to this browser session. Treat it like a public mailbox: never use it for banking, government or anything sensitive.
